# Style references: two sites the owner pointed to (4 Oct 2026)

Read from cattelanitalia.com/fr (home, identity) and roche-bobois.com/fr-MA (home, histoire, valeurs, Mah Jong
page). **Style analysis only. Never copy sentences**: both are copyrighted marketing text, and the Cattelan licence
is not signed yet. Owner feedback: the facts of the current site are fine, the style is not.

| | Current Cattelan Maroc site | cattelanitalia.com (brand, FR) | Roche Bobois Maroc |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opens a sentence with | the number or the object | the story or the feeling | the story, a place, a year, a name |
| Sells | measurements and options | family, wood, craft, form | heritage, designers, a way of living |
| Adjectives | almost none | many ("fascinant", "élégance naturelle") | few but strong ("audace", "sensible") |
| Sentence rhythm | short, clipped, lists, semicolons | long, descriptive, sensory | medium, confident, antitheses |
| Person | "nous" for service only | impersonal | "nous" and "pour nous" for values |
| Weakness | cold, no story, no sense of matter | French is a machine translation from Italian; stacked praise | cliché words (iconique, incontournable, raffiné) |

## What each does well (to borrow as method, not words)

- **Brand site:** anchors the brand in a person and a material ("Giorgio... porte dans son cœur le parfum du bois",
  from his father's workshop). Describes how a piece is built and why its form exists (Napoleon's ceramic base,
  Skorpio as the table that started a family of versions). Material and gesture, not just dimensions.
- **Roche Bobois:** each product page tells where the piece comes from (year, designer, the idea behind it), then
  its use. Values are stated as a position with contrast ("un équilibre entre raffinement et audace, entre héritage
  et réinvention"). Services are plain and polite.

## What to avoid from each

- Brand site: awkward French, literal translations, "parfaite", "fascinant", "harmonieux", "ductile".
- Roche Bobois: designer and couture name-dropping (Cattelan Maroc has no equivalent), superlatives, "iconique".
- Both: praise with no object. Keep a concrete noun (a material, a gesture, a place, a date) in every sentence.
