# Product marketing context: Cattelan Italia Maroc

Read first by the `copywriting` skill. It holds the facts, the audience, the rules and the target style for every
text of the Cattelan Italia Maroc website. Compiled 4 October 2026 from the repo docs, the live CMS, five press
articles, the brand's own site and the owner's decisions. Update this file when the owner decides something, not the
skills.

**Drafting rule: write from facts, never from the current text.** The current site style is the one the owner does
not like. Drafts are written from page briefs (`docs/copy-briefs/`), which list the slots to fill and the facts
allowed in each, not from the live copy. Do not read `docs/reference/current-site-copy-2026-10-04.md` while drafting.

## 2. What the site must do

Let a visitor discover the furniture, check an exact reference, or find the showroom. Contacting the shop is never a
required step. Each page answers one need (`docs/seo-page-map.md`). Primary actions: see a model, request a quote,
WhatsApp the showroom, book a visit, download the catalogue (demo PDF until the official one arrives).

## 3. Audience (hypotheses from the FAQ; no research done)

| Visitor | Typically wants to know | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowner in Casablanca | Is it authentic, which sizes and finishes, how long, delivery cost, can I see it first | Collections, models, Showroom, FAQ |

## 4. Positioning

- **One line (owner's decision):** "Le premier showroom exclusif Cattelan Italia au Maroc". Never "premier showroom"
  alone.
- **Proof that exists:** made in Italy; all brand finishes at the showroom; whole catalogue on order; installation

- **Proof that does not exist (do not claim):** official reseller status, warranty terms, after-sales, awards,

## 5. Voice and style

**Owner feedback, 4 Oct 2026: the information on the site is right, the style is not.** The old style is dry and
list-like. **Target style, chosen by the owner: story first, with a concrete material, gesture or place in every
sentence.** Method reference: `.agents/style-references.md` (never copy their sentences). Worked example:
`docs/copy-drafts/a-propos-2026-10-04.md`.

- Open with a person, a place, a year, a material or a gesture. Numbers follow where they help the reader decide.
- Every sentence holds a concrete noun (a material, an object, a place, a date, a gesture). Praise without an object
  says nothing.
- Calm, confident rhythm. Mix short and long sentences. One contrast per section at most, never in the form
  "ce n'est pas X, c'est Y".
- Story facts come only from `style-references.md` and `press-and-brand-sources.md`.
- Headlines name a place, a person, a material or the visitor's question; a short phrase with a verb in the third
  person is fine ("Cattelan Italia, de la Vénétie à Casablanca"). No orders, none built on "vous/votre".
- Buttons act: "Voir le modèle", "Demander un devis", "Prendre rendez-vous", "Nous écrire", "Télécharger le
  catalogue". WhatsApp buttons carry the WhatsApp icon and the accessible name "… sur WhatsApp".
- "Nous" for the showroom team. French from France.
- No AI tells: no contrast reveals, negation lists, trailing pile-on clauses, colon reveals, no em dashes in short
  copy, at most one list of three per section.
- A claim with no source is written `[NEED: ...]`, never invented.
- **Talk about the furniture, never about the website** (owner feedback, 4 Oct 2026): no "chaque modèle a sa page",
  "le site présente", "en ligne". No heading built on a count of models or families ("39 modèles en huit familles"),
  no inventory opener ("La famille compte cinq modèles"). Counts may appear later in a sentence, measurements anywhere.

## 6. Facts you may state

- Authentic, designed and made in Italy.
- Most collections can be customised (dimensions, materials, finishes, woods, ceramics, marbles, metals, fabric or
  leather). All brand finishes are shown at the showroom, as samples or on displayed pieces.
- Delay: "10 à 12 semaines au maximum après validation de la commande", custom pieces included. Never "fabriqué en
  10 à 12 semaines".
- Order steps: advice, detailed quote, validation and deposit, manufacture in Italy, delivery and installation.
- Deposit 50 % at validation, balance before delivery. Payment: card, bank transfer, cheque.
- A validated order is neither exchangeable nor refundable.
- Installation included. Delivery free in Casablanca; elsewhere in Morocco the cost depends on the destination (no
  figure). Delivery covers all of Morocco, notably Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech and Tanger.
- Distance orders: photos of samples, spec sheets and quote by WhatsApp; the order can be validated remotely.
- Prices are not shown: they depend on model, size and finishes and come in a detailed quote.
- Residential, hotel, restaurant and commercial projects are supported. 2D and 3D files, spec sheets and HD images
  are available on request, depending on the model (stated on the live site).
- Showroom facts: section 1.

## 8. Fact rules and the linter

- Count before writing a number (models, finishes, decors). No vague quantity where a number exists.
- No guarantee nobody can give. No production note in public copy, except the preview notice and the demo catalogue.
- A model's lead (also used on cards and meta) and its description never repeat each other: lead = identity + key
  numbers; description = construction, finishes, options. No sentence twice on a page.
- Specs: "L × P cm, hauteur H cm", always with units; "Piètement de 160 cm"; finish groups in French.
- Terms: buffet (not bahut); Botero base "revêtue d'argile"; Zuleika "habillée de cuir".
- Do not reuse the same sentence on Showroom, Professionnels and FAQ.

`node scripts/copy-lint.mjs <draft.md>` checks these rules and the style rules automatically. A draft goes to the
owner only with zero blocking findings. Allow-list: `scripts/copy-lint-allow.txt`.

## 10. Workflow for any copy task

1. Take the page brief in `docs/copy-briefs/` (slots to fill, facts per slot, fixed items). If none exists, build one.
2. Draft with `copywriting`, in a fresh context that has not read the live page. Output format: `docs/copy-briefs/README.md`.
3. Run `node scripts/copy-lint.mjs <draft>`. Fix every blocking finding.
4. Check each sentence has a source from section 6 or the press and brand file. Unsourced: `[NEED: ...]`.
5. Owner reviews the draft in `docs/copy-drafts/`. Nothing goes to the live CMS before approval
   (`docs/copy-apply-process.md`).
