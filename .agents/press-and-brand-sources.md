## Facts the three articles agree on

- First Cattelan Italia monobrand boutique in Morocco ("flagship"), in the Triangle d'Or, Casablanca,
  8-10 avenue du Dr Mohamed Sijilmassi.
- **400 m²** (all three). The articles do not say "two levels": that detail on the live site is still unsourced.
- Run by the **Berrada family, behind Diva Ameublement** (AE, Déco Actuelle).
- Shows dining rooms, living rooms **and bedrooms**, plus coffee tables, buffets, bookcases, lighting, **rugs and
  mirrors**. The live site has no bedrooms, beds or rugs yet (client questions 18 and 19 are open).
- Customisation: wood to leather, ceramic to metal and glass.
- Serves private clients, architects and decorators, for residential and professional projects.
- Brand founded 1979 by Giorgio and Silvia Cattelan, present in **more than 140 countries** (Shoelifer, Déco
  Actuelle, AE). Other aggregators say 104 or 150; use 140 only with a source, or avoid the number.

## Family and company story (Shoelifer; AE says "trois générations")

- 1960s: **Haj Driss Berrada**, upholstery fabrics. His son **Chafik** takes over. **Selma Berrada** and
  **Othmane Sefraoui** extend the business to furniture and decoration under the name **Diva Home**.
- The family had followed Cattelan Italia for several years. Reasons given: a catalogue wide enough to furnish a
  house from the living room to the bedroom, real freedom of customisation, and prices judged consistent for the range.
- Status: **Client OK needed** (questionnaire Q2 asks exactly this). Note the naming differs between sources:
  Diva Ameublement (AE, Déco Actuelle) and Diva Home (Shoelifer). Ask which is correct. Also the link between Racha
  Home (the legal operator) and the Berrada family is unconfirmed.
- Do not use the "qualité-prix" remark: it is a value claim nobody can guarantee.

## Wording: use with care

- The press says "arrivée officielle", "flagship", "boutique monomarque". The site may say **showroom monomarque**
  and **premier showroom exclusif**. It must **not** say "revendeur officiel" or "représentant officiel" until the
  trademark licence is signed (`docs/demande-licence-marque.md`). A magazine using the word does not authorise it.
- Press adjectives to avoid (several are on the banned list): mis en scène, généreuses, aérien, sculpturaux,
  signatures, univers, "un véritable intérieur".
- "Fabrication 100 % italienne" (Shoelifer, Déco Actuelle): the site says "fabriqué en Italie". Keep that wording.

## Official brand facts (cattelanitalia.com, identity page, read via summary: verify before publishing)

- Founded 1979 by Giorgio and Silvia Cattelan. Giorgio is the youngest of seven children, son of a carpenter from
  Thiene (Vicenza). First factory at **Carrè (VI) in 1982**. Started with small pieces in marble and glass; 1989
  widened to dining tables, chairs, shelving.
- Sons Paolo and Lorenzo joined; **Paolo Cattelan has led the company since 2014** (also in Shoelifer).
- **2011: acquisition of Arketipo**, a high-end upholstery maker in Florence. Verify the wording before using.
- Stated values: Made in Italy, quality, custom-made, flexibility, R&D, comfort, design, innovation, family.
- Materials the brand lists: transparent or decorated glass, fine woods, ceramics, lacquered or hand-brushed
  finishes, soft upholstery, fine leathers. Production relies on a close network of local partner companies.
- Tone of the brand's own text: warm, family-centred, craft-focused. Showroom copy stays plainer and more factual.
- Use for About-page facts (1979, Carrè, 1982, family succession). Do not reuse the brand's marketing sentences.
