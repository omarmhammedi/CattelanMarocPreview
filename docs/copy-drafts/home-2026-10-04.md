## Open points

- No [NEED] item: every slot is covered by the brief's fact bank.
- Lint: first run gave 1 review finding (many-commas on collections.text, a fronted material list then a trailing "exposé au showroom ou non"). It read as the old list style, so the sentence was rewritten from C3; C4 (materials) is no longer used in that slot. Second run: 0 blocking, 0 review.
- meta_description was cut from 160 to under 155 characters ("Le catalogue compte 39 modèles"). It says "catalogue", not "showroom", for the 39 models so it does not claim they are all on display.
- Word counts use spaces as separators; "d'Or", "s'y" and "rendez-vous" count as one word. Split at apostrophes and hyphens, showroom.text would reach 52 words (limit 50).
- catalogue.heading: K1 also says the file online is a demonstration until the final catalogue is ready. No home slot carries that notice; it is expected from the template's demonstration notice (functional microcopy, not briefed). Check that the notice shows next to the download button.
- brand.caption: the photos are provisional until the 9 October shoot. If the Skorpio photo changes (for example to Skorpio Keramik), the caption must follow the new photo.
- journal.text: "un buffet" stands for the guide on choosing between a buffet and a bookcase; the guides' sentence names five choices in one list (the only list in the block).
- showroom.text: S11 ("sur deux niveaux") left out on purpose; it is optional and no press article confirms it. The opening hours (Monday from 12:00) are left to the showroom page and the footer.
- collections.heading mixes a digit (39) and a word (huit), as French typography does for small numbers. If the site writes "8 familles" elsewhere, align it.
