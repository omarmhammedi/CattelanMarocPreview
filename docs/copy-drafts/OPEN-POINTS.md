## C. Wording choices for you

- **Brand name:** the home brand heading says "La maison Cattelan"; the alternatives keep "Cattelan Italia". (home)
- **Finish names in English:** Brushed Bronze, Brushed Grey, pearl, MIST, mix, iride, iron grey satiné are kept as the
  brand writes them. Translate or keep?
- **Guides:** each section ends with an instruction to the reader ("mesurez", "apportez"). Is that tone right?
- **Professionals:** "Nous suivons des projets…" (present tense, no client named). Acceptable, or too close to a claim
  of past work?
- **Consoles:** the collection page gives each console's exact depth (40, 45, 50 cm) beyond the brief's range. Keep or
  reduce to "38 à 50 cm"?
- **Skorpio:** "lignes obliques" (guide) vs "lignes croisées" (model data) for the same base.
- **Catalogue:** the meta description says "le catalogue" while the online PDF is still a demonstration.
- **SEO titles:** a few drafts offer alternatives (showroom with "meubles", FAQ with "Casablanca", "Commander…" vs
  "Commande et livraison · Cattelan Italia Maroc"). See each draft's Alternatives section.
