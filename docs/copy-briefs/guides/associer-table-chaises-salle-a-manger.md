## Fact bank

- **G2.1** Three rules: one strongly drawn piece, one finish in common, chairs that slide under the top.
- **G2.2** Simple chairs leave the base of Tyron Keramik or of Botero in view. Against a plainer table, the pleated back of Rhonda draws the eye.
- **G2.3** A finish in common: the lacquer of the legs, where both models offer it. Embossed titanium, bronze, graphite and black exist both on the base of Tyron Keramik and on the frame of Greta; Rhonda takes the same lacquers or a chrome.
- **G2.4** The link can also be a colour: a chair leather in the tone of a Portoro ceramic, or of Canaletto walnut. Compare the samples together at the showroom, in daylight.
- **G2.5** Widths: Greta 62 cm, Rhonda 63 cm, Zuleika 64 cm. To place the seats, count the chair width and the position of the table feet.
- **G2.6** Check the armrest height against the clearance under the top and its rails: a chair can suit by seat height and still not slide fully under.
- **G2.7** Covering by use: Rhonda exists in thin leather, faux leather, fabric and micro-nubuck, in more than a hundred shades. Compare the samples and the care notes of each finish, above all for a table used every day.
