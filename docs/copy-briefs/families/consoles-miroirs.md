## Models in this family

- **Glenn**: miroir aux bords courbés; au sol ou au mur
- **Cosmos**: miroir aux bords courbés; verre miroité fumé ou bronze; 120 ou 157 cm
- **Rado Keramik**: console tout en céramique; profondeur 38 cm; fixation murale
- **Nettuno**: console sur base ondulée; plateau en verre de 12 mm; fixation murale
- **Westin**: console sur lames d’acier croisées; plateau en verre de 12 mm; fixation murale

## Fact bank (family)

- **CO1** Three consoles and two mirrors: Westin on crossed steel blades, Nettuno on a wavy base, Rado Keramik all in ceramic; Cosmos and Glenn mirrors with curved edges.
- **CO2** The consoles are 38 to 50 cm deep (Rado Keramik 38 cm) and are fixed to the wall.
- **CO3** Mirror position: cut a paper template to the size of the mirror and tape it to the wall; consider switches and the objects planned on the console. [usage]
- **CO4** Glass tops of Westin and Nettuno are 12 mm thick. Cosmos and Glenn are mirrored glass; Cosmos smoked or bronze, 120 or 157 cm; Glenn on the floor or on the wall.

## Common facts

- **FAM1** All the brand's finishes are shown at the showroom, as samples or on displayed pieces (S3).
- **FAM2** The whole catalogue can be ordered, on display or not (C3).
- **FAM3** Made in Italy, delivered 10 to 12 weeks at most after validation, installation included, delivery free in Casablanca (SV5, SV6).
