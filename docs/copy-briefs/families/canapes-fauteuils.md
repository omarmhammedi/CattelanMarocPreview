## Fact bank (family)

- **CA1** Four sofas to compose, straight or corner: Craig (feather-filled back cushions), Douglas (high metal legs), Mykonos (rounded shapes), Sinatra (adjustable backs).
- **CA2** Ruby: straight sofa of 200 cm, exposed ash frame, with the matching Ruby Lounge armchair (81 cm wide).
- **CA3** Craig, Douglas, Mykonos and Sinatra come with removable fabric covers. A removable cover is not necessarily machine-washable: follow the care notice.
- **CA4** Leather ranges: Glove, Magnifica, Nabuk, Perfetto.
- **CA5** Draw the sofa modules, armchairs and coffee table on a scale plan; check door openings and the passages around the seats. [usage]
