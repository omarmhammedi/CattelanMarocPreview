## Fact bank (family)

- **CH1** Four models: Rhonda (pleated back), Greta (extended armrests), Miranda ML (enveloping shell), Zuleika (leather-clad). Rhonda and Greta also exist as stools.
- **CH2** Seat height: Miranda ML 47 cm, Zuleika 46 cm.
- **CH3** Widths: Greta 62 cm, Rhonda 63 cm, Zuleika 64 cm.
- **CH4** Check that the armrests pass under the tabletop and its rails. [usage]
- **CH5** Zuleika is covered in leather. Rhonda, Greta and Miranda ML come in leather, faux leather, fabric or micro-nubuck, in more than a hundred shades.
- **CH6** Compare the samples with the finish of the table at the showroom.

## Common facts

- **FAM1** All the brand's finishes are shown at the showroom, as samples or on displayed pieces (S3).
- **FAM2** The whole catalogue can be ordered, on display or not (C3).
- **FAM3** Made in Italy, delivered 10 to 12 weeks at most after validation, installation included, delivery free in Casablanca (SV5, SV6).
