## Models in this family

- **Dodo**: table basse en verre artistique
- **Adrian Wood**: deux essences de bois sur un même plateau; trois hauteurs
- **Albert Keramik**: table basse en céramique; trois hauteurs
- **Arena**: table basse ronde en bois laqué brossé; versions Arena Bond et Double Bond à plateaux pivotants

## Fact bank (family)

- **TA1** Four coffee tables to place alone or compose: Arena in lacquered brushed wood, Albert Keramik in ceramic, Adrian Wood in two woods, Dodo in artistic glass. From 28 to 54 cm high.
- **TA2** The Arena Bond and Double Bond versions have swivel tops.
- **TA3** Albert Keramik and Adrian Wood come in three heights.
- **TA4** Compare the height of the coffee table with that of the sofa seat; for a side table, with the armrest. [usage]
- **TA5** Mark the outline of the tops on the floor in the planned arrangement, and check you can sit, stand up and cross the room. [usage]

## Common facts

- **FAM1** All the brand's finishes are shown at the showroom, as samples or on displayed pieces (S3).
- **FAM2** The whole catalogue can be ordered, on display or not (C3).
- **FAM3** Made in Italy, delivered 10 to 12 weeks at most after validation, installation included, delivery free in Casablanca (SV5, SV6).
